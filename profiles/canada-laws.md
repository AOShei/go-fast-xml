# Profile `canada-laws`: Justice Canada federal laws

The consolidated acts and regulations (English `eng/acts`,
`eng/regulations`; French `fra/lois`, `fra/reglements`) and the annual
statutes of `github.com/justicecanada/laws-lois-xml`. English and French
files are separate files with the same elements; each is read on its own
and nothing pairs them.

`profiles/canada-laws.json` is generated by `TestCanadaGenerated`
(`profile_canada_gen_test.go`; `go test -run TestCanadaGenerated
-canada.update` rewrites it), so that the `lims:` attributes are kept
the same way on every rule.

**Selected by** the root `Statute`, `Regulation` or `Bill` (no namespace)
when the root declares `http://justice.gc.ca/lims` (with any prefix), or
when the file has a DOCTYPE whose public identifier starts with
`-//Justice Canada//`. A `Statute` with neither stays generic. In the dev
set, 218 of 243 files declare `lims` (every `Statute` and `Regulation`);
the 25 annual statutes (root `Bill`) declare no `lims` and use no `lims:`
attribute, and each has `<!DOCTYPE Bill PUBLIC "-//Justice Canada//DTD
Bill Exchange v2.5.0//EN" "BillExchange.dtd">`, which selects them. The
external DTD is not read (`input.external_reference`).

**Attributes kept** on the first block of each provision, heading and
container, as written: `lims:inforce-start-date`, `lims:inforce-end-date`,
`lims:lastAmendedDate`, `lims:enacted-date`, `lims:enactId`, `lims:fid`
(and `lims:pit-date`, `lims:current-date` where they occur), and under
`canada.<name>`: `type`, `in-force`, `status`, `target`, `style`,
`bilingual`, `spanlanguages`, `placement`, `salutation`,
`generate-in-text`, `orientation`, `list-item`, `id`. In-force state is
what `in-force` and the `lims:` dates declare: nothing is judged, and a
provision declared not in force is read like any other.

## Citing a block

`Loc.Anchor` is the `lims:id` of the element that makes the block (a
`Heading`, `MarginalNote`, `Text`, `Repealed`, table); a block made inside
a provision carries the provision's `lims:id` in `anchor_parent`.
`Marker` is the printed label (`1`, `(2)`, `(a)`, `PART 1`, `SCHEDULE`).

## Not kept

Counted in `xml.unmapped_attributes` (layout or ids of elements read for
their text): `format-ref`, `language-align`, `justification`, indents and
spacing, `include-in-TableOfProvisions`, `Leader@leader`/`@length`,
`AmendmentCitation@link` (a cell holds no link), the `lims:` attributes of
`HistoricalNoteSubItem` (a line of its note's block) and of the
`Identification` children read into `Extra`, CALS layout attributes.

Left to generic reading (not in the dev set): `TableOfProvisions`,
`CommentBlock`, `CommentInline`, `Ins`, `Del`, `Header`, `Footer`,
`ExplanatoryNote`, `Notice`.

## Versions

Dev samples: consolidated acts and regulations, English and French, as
published in 2025 (no DOCTYPE, `lims` declared), and annual statutes 2001
to 2024 (Bill Exchange DTD v2.5.0). The DTD read for this profile is the
repository's `regulation_web.dtd` 1.2.2. Not met in the dev set: MathML,
`Oath`, `QuotedText`, `TableOfProvisions`.
